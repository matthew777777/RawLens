⏺ I've made a fix, but it's based on my reading of the log and hasn't been tried
  on the Xiaomi 14T Pro. The unit tests pass, but they can't show whether the
  phone will actually focus now.

  What the log shows

  - The app sent the right requests. Each tap switched to tap-to-focus mode with
    the right spot on the sensor, and the camera started scanning.
  - The camera's autofocus kept failing. The first two tap scans and one
    tap-and-hold each searched for about 2 seconds and gave up. In between,
    continuous autofocus sat at the same distance (about 4 m) for more than 14
    seconds while the user moved things up close.
  - What these sessions have in common: the main camera (camera 0) was running
    with only the RAW stream and no preview stream (preview=none, combo RAW ONLY
    FULL). The app already knows some MediaTek phones misbehave in that setup:
    there's a check that adds a small hidden preview stream when they drop to
    about 20 fps. This phone passed that check at 30 fps, so it stayed RAW-only.
    My reading is that its autofocus doesn't get the data it needs without a
    preview stream. That fits the log, but I haven't been able to confirm it on
    the phone.
  - The first lens it opened (camera 3) only supports autofocus off, so it has
    no autofocus at all. That's normal for that lens, not a bug.

  The fix

  When the camera fails to focus on two taps in a row and hasn't focused
  successfully even once in that session, the app now treats the RAW-only
  session as the cause. It then uses the same remedy as the frame-rate check: it
  saves a per-camera setting saying this camera needs a preview stream, and
  restarts the camera session with the smallest hidden preview stream.

  - Changes:
    - AutoFocusPolicy.kt adds the two-miss rule (afLooksStarved,
      STARVED_MISS_STREAK = 2).
    - TouchFocus.kt counts misses in a row and resets the count on any
      successful focus or when the camera stops.
    - RawCameraController.kt handles the restart (onTouchAfStarved).
  - Limits: it only kicks in when the session is RAW-only and a preview option
    exists. It runs at most once per camera each time the app runs, so a phone
    that refuses the preview stream can't get stuck restarting. It also waits
    until no photo is being taken.
  - Phones where autofocus already works: a single successful focus rules this
    out for the session. Later misses, like a subject too close or with no
    detail, never trigger it.
  - Better logs: the tap-to-focus log lines now include the lens focus distance
    (fd=). If the problem comes back, the next log will show whether the lens
    moved at all during a scan.

  On the reporter's phone, this would have triggered at the second miss
  (14:12:05 in the log). The app would show "RECOVERING FOCUS" once, and after
  that camera 0 always opens with the preview stream.

  I added tests for the new rule in TouchFocusTest and AutoFocusPolicyTest; both
  suites pass (15 and 17 tests). Nothing is committed. The next step is a build
  on the 14T Pro: tap twice on something it can't focus, then check that the
  log shows "Touch AF starved raw-only… adding preview target" and that focus
  then works.

    up. On this phone that tap scan mode seems broken.
  - Continuous AF works fine. It only starts after the app sends a reset
    (CANCEL), and a failed tap's cleanup is what happened to send one. That's
    why you had to make one tap fail first. Right after the failed tap it
    focused from 0.33 to 6.89 D in under a second.
  - So the camera does have the data it needs to focus. My earlier idea of
    rebuilding the session with a hidden preview stream would only have
    restarted a session that wasn't the problem. It never kicked in anyway,
    because this log only had one failed tap.

  The fix

  1. Autofocus works from the start. About 0.5 s after each camera session
     opens, the app sends one reset (CANCEL) to wake continuous AF. On phones
     where continuous AF already runs, this just restarts it.
  2. Tap to focus uses continuous AF with the tapped spot. If a tap scan ends
     with the lens never having moved, the app:
     - retries the same tap straight away using continuous AF on the tapped
       spot, without showing "focus failed";
     - saves that choice for this camera on this device (not included in
       settings backup), so later taps go straight to the working method.

     With this method, a tap counts as focused once the camera reports it's in
     focus. A tap-and-hold also locks focus so it doesn't drift.
  3. Other phones are unaffected: a scan where the lens moves, or where the
     camera doesn't report lens position, is handled exactly as before.

  The changes are in TouchFocus.kt, AutoFocusPolicy.kt and
  RawCameraController.kt. In RawCameraController.kt I only removed my own
  earlier code by hand, so your uncommitted device-fault edits there are
  untouched. I added tests for the new paths, and both suites pass
  (TouchFocusTest 20 tests, AutoFocusPolicyTest 18). Nothing is committed.

  session start. Each time it started working in the logs, the camera had also
