RawLens changes: signed-log AP1 shaper, RGB8 LUT baking, trilinear CPU/GLES
sampling, sRGB and Display P3 encoding. This is a 100 nit SDR LUT
approximation, not an Academy-certified implementation. It does not
apply the older ACES fitted curve used by the video recording profile.
