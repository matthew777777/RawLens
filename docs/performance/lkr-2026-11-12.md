## Changes in this work

- Move live LK-R frame scoring to native RAW16 reads. Preserve the full-resolution
  green-gradient metric, float subtraction/addition, double accumulation order,
  Bayer phases, black levels, reference selection and rejection thresholds.
  Unsupported layouts retain the Kotlin path. Log selection time separately.
- In no-prior mode, coverage readback and final RGB readback have disjoint
  lifetimes. Share those two pairs of buffers, removing approximately 48 MiB
  across two workers with 512-pixel tiles at x2. Keep distinct buffers for DCT
  and external priors, and stable allocation slots across modes.
- Reuse finishing-filter luma/chroma scratch; retain one shadow-chroma plane
  instead of three; compute the independent highlight mask per pixel.
- Avoid copying each binned alignment frame before computing its median.

Pre-existing lossless RAW staging and experimental shader changes were retained.
No solver iteration counts, quality settings, image sizes or sample precision
were changed by this work.

## Step-one cutoff and follow-up research

At the user's request, step one ends at the validated exact-output memory and
frame-selection changes above. Subsequent scale-specialization, workgroup-size
and local-address-hoisting experiments were restored to the step-one baseline
before committing. They are not enabled by this commit. Existing off-by-default
half-tap experiments from the initial working tree are retained as explicitly
experimental code; the measurements above use full-precision defaults.

Follow-up profiling on Mali-G615 (MT6878), eight-frame 512-pixel x2 crop, found
about 719 ms in fused residual/scatter out of 1896 ms total GPU time. Mali Offline
Compiler 2026.5 estimates that kernel is load/store-bound. Specialization can
reduce loop work but increased its predicted register allocation from 32 to 64;
it needs device benchmarking before adoption. The Mac specialization experiment
showed no clear kernel improvement and increased cold pipeline setup time.

Primary references for follow-up work:

- [Arm GPU Best Practices, revision 3.4](https://documentation-service.arm.com/static/67a62b17091bfc3e0a947695?token=),
  compute workgroups and specialization guidance, chapters 9 and 10.
- [Mali Offline Compiler](https://developer.arm.com/Tools%20and%20Software/Mali%20Offline%20Compiler),
  register, spill and functional-unit cost reports.
- [Qualcomm Snapdragon OpenCL Programming and Optimization](https://docs.qualcomm.com/doc/80-NB295-11/80-NB295-11_REV_C_Qualcomm_Snapdragon_Mobile_Platform_Opencl_General_Programming_and_Optimization.pdf),
  architecture guidance on cache locality, atomic contention and limiting compute
