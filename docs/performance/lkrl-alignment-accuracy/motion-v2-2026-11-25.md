# LK-R Lite motion v2 (2026-11-25)

Default since 2026-11-25 (uncommitted at writing). Off: `debug.lkr.align2=0` (phone) or `LKRL_ALIGN2=0`; off is identical to the old version in score on the checked scenes (fence, water, HDR; 1x and 2x).

**Not verified: phone speed and memory** (phone testing paused), so the production gate is not passed.

## What changed
- **Patch seed:** block offsets re-searched only where the residual exceeds noise (`lkrl_patchseed.cpp`).
- **8 px warp for the merge:** the 16 px field resampled to 8 px, with offsets measured per block (`upfine.comp`).
- **2x support step:** weakly covered green pixels move with the local mean (comb in moving water; `cdupd.comp`).

## The 8 px fix
The first 8 px version lost on fence and water (1x) and night motion (2x). Ablation: 8 px caused it, not patch seed.
1. Resampling to nodes at 4 and 12 cut the field's peaks by 25 %, even where nothing was corrected. Compensated by 1/16 of the second difference at the nearest coarse node. With no corrections accepted this alone is +0.16 dB (fence) and +0.65 dB (foliage) at 1x.
2. Measured offsets were added to a field that the motion step had since moved. They are now used as measured (tiny effect).

Tighter acceptance thresholds did not help.

**Trade-off:** on real bursts the peak compensation gives back part of the 8 px gain (a smoother field scores better there); b141 and b170 at 2x are up to 2 % (b141) and 0.4 % (b170) worse than old. Half compensation sits between, but left fence / water at -0.12 dB and lost 3 dB on one dev repeat pair. The metric also rewards blur in moving water.

## Rejected
- **H step** (host block solve): real static scene b141 +4.6 % error. Removed.
- **Catmull-Rom forward model** (`LKRL_CUBIC`): 1x 3-9 % worse on real static areas and -5.9 dB on repeating patterns; 2x 1-4 % better on real, kept as an off-by-default experiment. The synthetic scenes are themselves sampled with a bicubic kernel and favour it.
