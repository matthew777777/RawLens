# RawLens 2.0.69

Highlights from the original 2.0 release and updates through 2.0.69.

## Photos and exposure

- **MFSR handheld super-resolution**, faster HDR+ merges and darktable highlight reconstruction.
- **PROGRAM** exposure based on libcamera, Magic Lantern **ETTR**, Active D-Lighting, RAW zebras, false color and spot metering.
- **RAWR Capture Sharpening**, enabled by default with four strengths; improved wavelet denoise with independent Strength, Detail and Luma controls.
- **Ultra HDR JPEG, JPEG XL and optional lossless JPEG XL DNG** output.
- More reliable tap/hold autofocus and improved RAW focus peaking with noise, blur and clipping rejection.

## Video

- Lossless **`.craw` RAW Video**, audio meters and RGB binning.
- **Direct Log is now Encoded Video**, with existing settings and LUT selections migrated.
- Rec.709, S-Log3/S-Gamut3.Cine, HLG and ACES profiles, plus user LUT support.
- Fixed the redundant eight-second stop delay; improved save-error reporting and recording diagnostics.
- **Video RCD has been removed.** Video offers MHC (default) and Super pixel; old RCD selections use MHC. **Photo RCD remains available.**

## Controls and reliability

- Refreshed Material controls, adaptive quick panels, swipe handling and clearer layouts.
- Optional fresh-GPS tags for photos and MP4, plus improved lens/capture metadata.
- Better camera-session fallback, per-lens vendor profiles and RAW-size handling.
- TinyDNG fallback for incompatible cropped RAW sizes; recoverable Android DNG writer errors.
- RAW recording tolerates missing white balance and optional metadata.
- More reliable application logs on devices with restricted system logging.
- Earlier MFSR save/startup-resource fixes; the old RAW-SR pipeline and GALOSH denoiser were retired.

## Before publishing

Host checks passed: **1,826 tests, zero failures/errors, six skips**, plus release Kotlin compilation. Affected-Xiaomi fixes and sustained recording still need device validation; an earlier MHC recording check failed its frame-count assertion. The replacement video RCD is not included.

Sony color-chart validation remains pending; S-Cinetone is deferred. Recording LUTs are not shown in the idle RAW viewfinder.
