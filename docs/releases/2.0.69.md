# RawLens 2.0.69

Consolidated notes for the **2.0 series through 2.0.69**: the original 2.0.0
feature set plus the subsequent photo-processing, autofocus, focus-peaking,
controls, metadata and recording-reliability updates. The original 2.0 release
covered changes since 1.0.192; the later updates below cover changes since
2.0.27.

**Important video change:** the previous packed video RCD implementation has
been removed. Encoded Video now offers **MHC (default)** and **Super pixel**;
existing RCD video selections migrate to MHC. **Still-photo RCD remains
available.** The planned replacement video RCD is not included in this release.

## The 2.0 foundation, carried forward

### Exposure and RAW exposure tools

- **PROGRAM auto exposure** is based on libcamera's Raspberry Pi algorithm,
  including filtered convergence, highlight-aware metering and flicker handling.
  Highlight-weighted metering considers all color channels.
- **ETTR** follows Magic Lantern's auto-ETTR approach: default headroom is
  1 EV below clipping, adjustable over 0.5–2 EV, with midtone/shadow noise limits.
- **Active D-Lighting**, from Off to Extra high, combines highlight protection
  with shadow lifting through one control.
- **RAW zebras, false color and a RAW spot meter** report clipping and exposure
  from sensor values rather than judging the rendered preview.
- **Flash precapture** waits for the camera's metering sequence to enter and
  leave precapture, following Open Camera's two-phase approach. PROGRAM and
  ETTR offer Off/On flash choices.

### Photo capture, reconstruction and output

- **MFSR handheld multi-frame super-resolution** merges pre-shutter frames into
  one photo using natural hand motion for subpixel sampling, rejecting blurred
  frames and frames captured while focus or exposure is still adjusting.
  MFSR replaces the previous RAW super-resolution pipeline.
- **Per-lens vendor profiles** support typed OEM request/session keys,
  including device-specific operating-mode configuration, with bounded fallback,
  strict import and atomic profile edits. A profile does not guarantee that an
  undocumented sensor mode is supported by a particular device.
- **HDR+ merge optimizations** preserve the tested output. The original 2.0
  release measured Fast merge at 4.6 → 3.5 seconds on its test phone; this is a
  historical device-specific benchmark, not a universal timing guarantee.
- **darktable inpaint-opposed highlight reconstruction** operates on RAW CFA
  data before demosaic. Further neutral-basis corrections are listed below.
- Output options include **Ultra HDR JPEG**, **JPEG XL photos** and optional
  **lossless JPEG XL DNG** compression.
- Capture-time aperture, focal length, focus distance, flash, exposure
  compensation and other metadata are retained in DNG/JPEG/JPEG XL, including
  merged and offline-developed output.
- Earlier reliability fixes address MFSR JPEG+DNG save crashes and allow a
  multi-frame capture to continue when an individual RAW frame fails.

### RAW Video and encoded profiles

- **RAW Video uses Cinematic RAW Studio `.craw`**, with lossless compression,
  audio and stereo level meters. Codec choices preserve the current and legacy
  formats; compressed size depends on the selected codec and scene.
- **RAW-video RGB binning** records half-resolution RGB rather than a Bayer
  mosaic that desktop tools would demosaic again, avoiding that additional
  demosaic stage and its color-fringe artifacts.
- Encoded Video (formerly Direct Log) includes the **ACES display look** and
  retains the Rec.709, S-Log3/S-Gamut3.Cine and HLG profile choices documented
  for the 2.0 series.
- The original 2.0.0 notes advertised real-time **video RCD**. That implementation
  has since been removed; its historical 4K24/1440p30/1080p30 claims are
  superseded by the removal notice above and do not describe 2.0.69.

### Camera workflow and earlier cleanup

- The 2.0 interface introduced clearer active-feature tags, a revised lens
  selector, a video quick panel with torch and gallery access from the camera.
  The subsequent Material-control and layout changes are listed below.
- The former RAW super-resolution pipeline was replaced by MFSR, and the
  **GALOSH denoiser** was removed.
- Release builds use **R8** shrinking. Upload the matching mapping archive with
  the production APKs so release crash traces can be decoded.

## Updates after 2.0.27

### Photo processing

- Add **RAWR Capture Sharpening**, enabled by default, with Low, Medium, Sharp
  and Sharpest strength presets. It runs after full-resolution demosaic and
  before white balance/color conversion and optional wavelet denoise.
- Apply Capture Sharpening to supported Bayer photo-development paths,
  including JPEG/JPEG XL, reconstructed CFA, merged photos and super-resolution
  RGB output. Quad-Bayer processing keeps its separate path. Strength is
  preserved when the feature is disabled and re-enabled.
- Align profiled wavelet denoise with the pinned RAWR implementation. Strength
  now spans **0–8**, Detail is exposed over **0–1.8**, and Luma is independently
  adjustable. Scale selection covers 1–7 without a duplicate zero position.
- Correct wavelet noise normalization to use the two green CFA sites and the
  appropriate black-level contribution. Missing or invalid noise profiles
  bypass denoise instead of using invented profile constants.
- Keep sharpening and wavelet settings frozen with each capture and available
  to offline development/merge settings and preference backups.
- Correct opposed-highlight reconstruction reference balancing so it uses the
  camera neutral basis without applying white balance twice.
- Improve camera-linear processing/readback and processing-context cleanup
  around the sharpening, denoise and merged-development stages.

### Autofocus and focus peaking

- Replace the previous photo autofocus recovery heuristics with one Camera2
  tap/hold controller. Correlate AF results with the active request, trigger
  frame and session so late results from an earlier tap cannot publish a new
  focus verdict.
- Restore continuous-picture focus after a temporary tap lock expires; retain
  a long-press lock until explicitly released. Report failed/inconclusive scans
  without a false successful-focus badge.
- Remove learned dead-AUTO verdicts and speculative focus-mode retry loops.
  Use only modes and metering-region capabilities advertised by the camera.
- Rebuild photo and RAW-recording focus peaking around the same RAW detail
  detector, with native processing and a bounded background worker.
- Improve weak sharp-edge detection, noise/blur rejection and clipped-sample
  masking. Draw marks at detected edge positions and invalidate stale results
  after focus changes or camera shutdown.
- Keep peaking independent of HAL autofocus success and ISP preview pixels.
  A featureless surface still cannot provide optical-focus evidence.

### Interface and controls

- Refresh settings and controls with Material styling, consistent touch sizes,
  updated input fields, switches and button presentation.
- Add adaptive quick-panel/tile layouts, text fitting and panel swipe handling.
  Refine photo/video quick controls, framing/focus overlays and layout sizing.
- Update the welcome screens and main-camera layout for the revised controls.
- Rename **Direct Log** to **Encoded Video** throughout the UI, implementation,
  shaders and tooling. The profile tile is now **PROFILE**, since output can
  be Rec.709 or one of the supported log/HDR profiles.
- Migrate existing recording settings, LUT selections/intensities and crash
  markers. Imported preference backups normalize the old namespace; current
  values take precedence.

### Encoded Video

- Remove the disputed packed RCD shaders, generated/portable shader binaries,
  native backend/resources, JNI wrapper, reference tests and build generators.
  Retain MHC and Super pixel as the current recording choices.
- Fix a redundant shutdown wait: after output EOS was already received, the
  recorder could wait for a second EOS marker for about eight seconds.
  Preserve EOS state through the drain/tail loops and flush queued audio before
  declaring drain completion.
- Improve recording failure reporting and retain explicit save failures in the
  UI. Log total stop duration to help diagnose slow finalization.
- Retain the compatible staging/plane-copy delivery path for codec input.
  The removed backend's direct GPU-writer path is disabled.
- Add bounded startup diagnostics for RAW geometry, Bayer samples, output
  P010 samples, destination plane strides/capacities and sampled copy checks.
  Diagnostic readbacks are limited to the initial frames.

### Capture metadata and location

- Add optional **MP4 recording location** from the fresh GPS fix available at
  recording start; retain it through stabilized output.
- Use monotonic fix age, expire fixes after two minutes and clear cached fixes
  when collection stops or location access changes. Captures without a fresh
  fix omit location metadata. Location remains opt-in.
- Improve EXIF/DNG lens descriptions using the device identity, optical 35 mm
  equivalent and aperture. Record inferred lens make without claiming a
  particular optical supplier.
- Preserve capture-time metadata consistently through delayed/offline output
  and RAW-video metadata serialization.

### Camera compatibility and RAW saving

- Prefer a normal advertised HD ISP preview when a camera needs the preview
  compatibility fallback; RAW-only remains the normal initial photo session.
- Retain intermediate advertised cropped RAW sizes during session fallback,
  and apply requested profile preview rates only through supported ranges.
- Improve per-camera fault recovery accounting so a working fallback does not
  reset the exhausted recovery budget of a failing route.
- Normalize saved RAW-size strings, including the multiplication-sign display
  form, and correct capture-queue capacity accounting.
- Avoid Android DngCreator for RAW dimensions that do not match its accepted
  sensor extents; select **TinyDNG** for incompatible/cropped geometry.
- Convert platform DNG metadata assertions into recoverable save errors rather
  than allowing them to terminate the application.
- Prevent missing/invalid RAW-video white-balance metadata from aborting a
  valid recording. Prefer valid camera neutral, then usable camera gains,
  then a same-frame RAW estimate. If no estimate is possible, omit the neutral
  and record its unavailable status rather than inventing unity white balance.
- Omit missing/non-finite optional CRAW metadata while preserving strict
  image/result timestamp matching.

### Diagnostics, documentation and source notices

- Capture application log messages directly throughout a logging session,
  independently of OEM logd behavior. Mirror logs periodically even when
  system log output stops arriving.
- Expand regression tests and retain processing/parity, AF/peaking, metadata,
  recording and migration audit evidence. Update the user guide and privacy
  statement for Encoded Video and location behavior.
- Update third-party notices and document removal of the former video RCD
  dependency. Affected source histories were rewritten and releases 2.0.0 and
  2.0.27 withdrawn to private drafts. These actions do not constitute a finding
  about ownership or resolution of the existing issue.

## Validation and remaining limitations

- Host verification: 1,826 unit tests, 0 failures, 0 errors and
  6 skips; release Kotlin compilation passed. Earlier processing/AF/peaking evidence is device/build-specific;
  it is not a new full-device acceptance run of 2.0.69.
- The cropped-DNG and missing-WB recording fixes require a retest on the
  affected Xiaomi routes. They do not establish DCG support or 60 fps support.
- An earlier cleaned-tree MHC recording test failed its muxed-frame-count
  assertion. Further device tests were deferred at the user's request;
  sustained recording acceptance remains incomplete.
- This release does **not** claim a new RCD backend or newly verified stable
  1080p30, 1440p30 or 4K24 RCD recording. Those are requirements for future work.
- The memory-warning investigation did not establish a persistent leak, so no
  general memory-leak fix is claimed.
- Physical Sony color-chart acceptance remains pending; S-Cinetone is deferred.
- LUT monitoring is available while recording; the idle RAW viewfinder does
  not show that recording LUT.
- Historical baked-LUT recording checks reported both passing short runs and
  a failing longer cadence run. They are not current sustained-performance
  acceptance for 2.0.69 or its remaining video choices.

Fresh APKs must be built from tag **2.0.69** with the established production
signing key before upload/publication. Version code: **2069**.
