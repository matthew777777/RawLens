# RawLens 2.3.43

Changes since **2.2.86**.

## New Lucas–Kanade Reloaded merging

The new **LK-R (Lucas–Kanade Reloaded Lite)** engine is redesigned with mobile GPUs in mind.

- **Up to around 3× faster in measured mobile runs:** an eight-frame Mali-G615 burst fell from **52.9 to 19.7 seconds at 1×**, and **120.7 to 48.0 seconds at 2×**. Results vary by device, scene and settings.
- Improve motion handling and reduce checker patterns, colour bands and edge zipper while retaining static detail.
- Add **PhotonCamera-based KernelNet denoising, on by default**. Low-noise scenes skip it; denoising increases as noise rises. An on/off switch is available, and saved choices are preserved.
- Prewarm Vulkan pipelines to reduce first-merge setup. Support up to **30 frames in 2× modes**, subject to camera capacity and memory.

## Faster MFSR and cleaner merges

- Optimize the mobile MFSR pipeline and add **MFSR Lab** tuning and a **2× super-resolution** switch.
- Improve detail at 1×/2× and reduce tile seams, boxy contours and Bayer-phase zipper through revised reconstruction and motion rejection.
- Default offline merges to JPEG, add quality controls and burst sharing, preserve staged-take orientation, and fix developed-output filenames.
- Default HDR+ to HQ with strength 3; automatic strength/quality remain selectable and saved choices are respected.
- Improve desktop noise estimation, colour-difference controls and DCT prior diagnostics.

## Photo output and camera fixes

- Make **Basic tone mapping the default**, with improved highlight preservation. AgX remains selectable; existing saved choices are retained.
- Add two-radius chroma cleanup, improve RAW white-balance fallback and lens-shading transport, and fix colour loss in flat blue/purple areas.
- Improve autofocus recovery in RAW-only sessions and add a bounded retry for abandoned tap scans.
- Extend sequential two-exposure RAW HDR to manual RAW lenses and rooted **DCG14**. This mode has no alignment or motion correction.
- Add experimental root-only lapis **200→50 MP** capture with LinearRaw DNG and developed output. Requires supported firmware; conversion is slow and burst/ZSL are unavailable.

## Preview, video and settings

- Reduce Encoded Video preview processing work and pause the camera behind Settings.
- Continue work on gyro stabilization v2 with L1 smoothing and self-calibration.
- Add **ZIP export/import for lens profiles**, with confirmation before replacing existing profiles.
- Rename the denoising/sharpening page to **Details**, fix narrow-screen update actions, and keep **MFSR / LK-R** names consistent throughout the app.
