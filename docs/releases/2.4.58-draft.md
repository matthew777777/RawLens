# RawLens 2.4.58

**Draft — not published.** Phone testing for this build is still pending.

The latest RawLens features LK-R next-generation multi-frame super-resolution, alongside open-source "SABRE 2.0" MFSR (Jamy-L CUDA → Numba → RawLens Vulkan).

Beyond conventional mobile photography. 📸

## Main features

- **LK-R next-generation multi-frame super-resolution.** Sharper detail from handheld bursts. LK-R Lite aligns frames more precisely, and an optional NPU denoiser with a trained prior cleans up what's left. The new finish stage keeps fine static detail, and rejection works harder on moving areas.
- **SABRE 2.0 MFSR.** Open-source multi-frame super-resolution, ported from Jamy-L CUDA through Numba to RawLens Vulkan. The merge runs as an async GPU pipeline, lining up the next frame while the current one merges. On a Mali-G615 phone, an 8 × 12.5 MP night burst merges in 3.9–4.4 s, down from 14–17 s, with peak memory down from about 1.7 GB to 1.3 GB.
- **HDR+ runs faster on phones.** On a Mali-G615 phone with 8 frames at 12.5 MP, Fast mode drops from about 3.15 s to 1.25 s, and HQ from 6.1 s to about 3.8 s of GPU time. The output is bit-identical to the previous build. Frames stream through double-buffered uploads, so the GPU stays busy while the next frames load.
- **Faster JPEG.** JPEG encoding runs in parallel, so saves finish sooner. Ultra HDR shares the same encoder.
- **New denoise.** A CFA-domain Vulkan DCT denoiser built for raw sensor data. It works on the Bayer pattern itself, before demosaicing.
- **New denoise.** [Second denoise item — to be specified.]
- **JPEG XL and Ultra HDR fixes.** Highlights and gain maps are written more reliably, so HDR output looks the way you shot it in both formats. Clipped highlights are rebuilt from unclipped neighbours, and DNG black and white levels are repaired before saving.
- **And much more:**
  - **Video with sound.** Encoded video recordings now include an AAC audio track.
  - **Black & white, Quincunx mode.** A new B&W option with a 45-degree green lattice for sharper mono detail. Detail and Clean are unchanged.
  - **Develop queue has its own look.** It starts from your camera JPEG look and remembers your edits. B&W DNG import works in the queue too.
  - **Smoother manual controls.** Sliders update live while you scrub and lock in on release. The preview keeps flowing while you adjust focus, with focus peaking and the magnifier updating live.
  - **Exposure that knows your scene.** Exposure monitoring uses scene measurements, camera metadata and learned noise.
  - **RAW compatibility** on Samsung and Xiaomi Ultra RAW 50 phones, with a custom RAW reader size and better autofocus and metering.
  - **Still white balance that keeps up.** When the camera is still hunting for white balance, RawLens uses a RAW-based estimate.
  - **Full-size RAW** and **optional capture sharpening** for MFSR.
  - **A cleaner viewfinder.** It runs on a GPU import path with a NEON CPU fallback, and the saved frame's brightness matches the preview.
  - **Photo cube LUTs everywhere.** Your look shows in the viewfinder, on stills, and in offline merge exports.
  - **DNG by default is TinyDNG v3.** Automatic mode still tries Android's DngCreator first.
  - **More reliable video.** The recording monitor works on more phones, and the encoder waits for pending frames before closing the file.
  - **A tidier capture screen.** Redesigned capture dial and quick controls, with Settings grouped into General, Burst, Exposure, Video, About and Debug. "HDR" is now "Brackets."
  - **vivo 50 MP RAW** for still shots on supported devices.

Experimental options stay off until you turn them on in Settings.
