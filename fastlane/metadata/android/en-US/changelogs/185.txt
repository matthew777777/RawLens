First public release: a photography-first Camera2 RAW camera.

- Full-resolution RAW_SENSOR stills as DNG; JPEG / JPEG+DNG / DNG-only
- AUTO, PROGRAM, ZSL, and MANUAL exposure modes; tap/drag AF+AE targets
- RAW zero-shutter-lag ring with motion-aware frame picking
- HDR brackets, HDR+ merge, and RAW super-resolution (Linear/Mosaic)
- AMaZE/RCD demosaic; AgX Base/Golden/Punchy JPEG looks
- GALOSH and wavelet denoise options
- Direct Log video (4K P010 HEVC) and RAW video capture
- Histogram, waveform, focus peaking, horizon/level guide
- Per-lens DNG calibration overrides; settings backup/restore
