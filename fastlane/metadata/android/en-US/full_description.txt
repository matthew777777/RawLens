RawLens is a photography-first, open-source Android camera built around a
scene-referred RAW pipeline. It captures full-resolution Camera2 sensor data
as DNG files, develops calibrated RAW JPEG, JPEG XL and Ultra HDR output, and
records lossless RAW video and Direct Log video, with direct exposure controls
and a minimal portrait-oriented viewfinder. No cloud sync, accounts, ads, or
analytics.

Features:
- Full-resolution RAW_SENSOR stills saved as DNG, JPEG, JPEG+DNG or JPEG XL;
  optional lossless JPEG XL DNG
- AUTO, PROGRAM, ZSL and MANUAL modes; single-frame and six-frame bursts
- PROGRAM auto exposure ported from libcamera's Raspberry Pi AGC; ETTR after
  Magic Lantern; Active D-Lighting
- RAW zebras, false colour and a RAW spot meter; focus peaking
- RAW zero-shutter-lag capture from a bounded pre-shutter buffer
- MFSR handheld multi-frame super-resolution, HDR+ burst merge, HDR brackets,
  and offline merges of saved bursts
- darktable inpaint-opposed highlight reconstruction
- RAW development with AMaZE or RCD and AgX Base, Golden and Punchy looks
- RAW video in the lossless .craw (Cinematic RAW Studio) container with
  32-bit float audio, gyro/accelerometer tracks and optional RGB binning
- Direct Log video (Rec.709, S-Gamut3.Cine/S-Log3, HLG, ACES) with real-time
  RCD demosaic and user .cube LUTs for monitoring or baking
- Wavelet and AI (RawNIND) RAW denoise
- Tap/drag focus and metering targets, face-priority AF/AE
- Per-camera DNG calibration overrides and per-lens vendor keys
- Live histogram/waveform, grid, level guide, timer, torch, OIS toggle
- Optional GPS geotagging embedded in saved photos (off by default)

Requirements: Android 11 or newer, and a rear camera that advertises the
Camera2 RAW capability. RAW support is device- and lens-dependent.

F-Droid build note: this build compiles from source only. The optional
Halide-accelerated viewfinder rung falls back to another engine and the
HDR/scope meters use their Kotlin fallbacks; the neural features (AI denoise,
FlowNet alignment, KernelNet MFSR kernel) run on CPU instead of GPU. Capture,
DNG/JPEG/JPEG XL output, HDR+, MFSR, wavelet denoise and video are fully
available. In-app update checks are disabled; updates come through F-Droid.
