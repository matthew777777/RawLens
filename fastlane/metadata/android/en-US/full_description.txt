RawLens is a photography-first, open-source Android camera built around a
scene-referred RAW pipeline. It captures full-resolution Camera2 sensor data
as DNG files and develops calibrated RAW JPEG output, with direct exposure
controls and a minimal portrait-oriented viewfinder. No gallery, cloud sync,
accounts, ads, or analytics.

Features:
- Full-resolution RAW_SENSOR stills saved as DNG, JPEG, or JPEG+DNG
- Single-frame and six-frame burst release modes
- AUTO, PROGRAM (custom RAW-driven AE), ZSL, and MANUAL exposure modes
- Manual ISO, shutter speed, white balance, and exposure compensation
- Center-weighted, frame-average, and spot AE metering
- Optional RAW zero-shutter-lag capture from a bounded pre-shutter buffer
- Tap/drag focus and exposure metering targets, face-priority AF/AE
- HDR brackets merged into one HDR DNG, plus HDR+ and RAW super-resolution
- RAW JPEG development with AgX Base, Golden, and Punchy looks
- Per-camera DNG calibration overrides (levels, noise profile, color matrices)
- Live histogram, grid, horizon/level guide, timer, torch, OIS toggle
- Optional GPS geotagging embedded in saved DNG/JPEG files (off by default)

Requirements: Android 10 or newer, and a rear camera that advertises the
Camera2 RAW capability. RAW support is device- and lens-dependent.

F-Droid build note: this build compiles from source only. The optional
Halide-accelerated viewfinder rung falls back to the legacy engine, the
HDR/scope meters use their Kotlin fallbacks, and the prebuilt neural-network
helpers are inactive (no AI denoise; FlowNet alignment uses the identity
fallback). Capture, DNG/JPEG output, HDR+, super-resolution, and GALOSH and
wavelet denoise are fully available. In-app update checks are disabled;
updates come through F-Droid.
